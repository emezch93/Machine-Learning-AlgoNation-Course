Privacy

This course application has no backend and no account system. It does not collect, transmit, or store your data on any server.

What Is Stored

Lesson progress, completed items, checklist state, and your last-viewed lesson are saved using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and browser only. Clearing your browser data or local storage will reset your progress.

What Is Not Collected

The app does not use analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. It does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information. It does not process payments.

Third-Party Links

Links to the AI Tutor (codeventdigital.site/chat.html) and the main CodeVent Digital site take you to a separate service outside this application. That service has its own data handling, covered by its own policy on codeventdigital.site.
